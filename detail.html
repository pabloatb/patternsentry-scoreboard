Resolved calls

How a call is scored. A zone is touched when a bar trades into it. It is a hit when price then reacts 1R beyond the zone (R = zone height, floored at half an average bar) and a fail when price pushes 0.5R through it; whichever comes first. The touch bar itself can only fail. Zones older than the age limit expire and are excluded from the hit rate. Target zones (harmonic A-D band, Elliott ABC band) are hit when reached and failed when the stop is taken first. AI zones follow the same rules.
Scores describe past price reactions at detected levels. They are not a prediction or a recommendation. Trading futures, forex and CFDs involves substantial risk of loss.
