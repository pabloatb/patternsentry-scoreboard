Scoring rules. A zone is touched when a bar trades into it; hit on a 1R reaction beyond it (R = zone height, floored at half an average bar), failed on a 0.5R push through it, expired after the age limit and excluded. Objective zones (harmonic A-D band, Elliott ABC band) are hit when reached and failed when the stop is taken first; both in one bar counts as failed. A touch bar can only fail, never hit. AI zones are scored with the same rules.
Trust filter. Every new call is classed at birth as trusted, unproven or muted from the pooled record of its source on that chart, market or everywhere (95% Wilson bounds, 30+ resolved calls). Muted calls are not delivered and are not in the tables above; they are counted in the line under each table. AI zones are labelled but always delivered.
Scores describe past price reactions at detected levels and are not a prediction or a recommendation. Trading futures and CFDs involves substantial risk of loss.
