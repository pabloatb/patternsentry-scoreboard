Scoring rules. A zone is touched when a bar trades into it; hit on a 1R reaction beyond it (R = zone height, floored at half an average bar), failed on a 0.5R push through it, expired after the age limit and excluded. Target zones (harmonic A-D band, Elliott ABC band) are hit when reached and failed when the stop is taken first; both in one bar counts as failed. A touch bar can only fail, never hit. AI zones are scored with the same rules. Trust filter: each new call is classified from the pooled record of calls of its kind (same source, market, timeframe; wider buckets when thin): trusted when the lower 95% bound is at or above 45%, muted when the upper bound is below 40%, unproven otherwise. Muted calls are drawn but not sent, and still scored here.
Scores describe past price reactions at detected levels and are not a prediction or a recommendation. Trading futures and CFDs involves substantial risk of loss.
